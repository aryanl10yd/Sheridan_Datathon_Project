# Important limitations

The requested CSV datasets were not attached. Demo vulnerability records, historical signals, assets, device and identities are fictional and labeled; they are not live CVE, EPSS, KEV or exploit data. Any real analysis is bounded by the schemas, provenance, timestamps and labels present in user-supplied data. No 2026 scoreboard is training data. A predictive model is unavailable until sufficiently timestamped historical features and outcomes are supplied and validated. No evaluation metrics may be inferred from demonstration examples.

IBM Granite inference requires a compatible endpoint and credentials. Without them, deterministic demonstration responses are explicitly labeled and do not constitute Granite-generated intelligence. The demo does not guarantee the authenticity of human identities or hardware, is not certified, cannot be considered a security control and is not connected to any operational system.
